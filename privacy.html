Austin Crane AI Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026. This policy describes the intended operation of Austin Crane's SMS assistant and this informational website. The SMS service is currently being configured.

Information processed

When you use the SMS service, it processes your phone number, message contents, timestamps, delivery information, and information you voluntarily provide. Conversation history and relevant details may be stored by Hermes to provide context and relay your inquiries to Austin.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver and respond to messages, understand inquiries, support follow-up by Austin, honor messaging preferences, troubleshoot the service, and prevent misuse.

Service providers and AI processing

Twilio processes phone numbers, message contents, and delivery data to provide SMS. Hermes runs the assistant. Message contents and relevant conversation context are sent through OpenRouter to the selected AI inference provider to generate replies; the current model is GLM 5.3 Flash. These providers may process and retain data under their applicable service terms and policies. Austin can review the conversations. This service does not offer end-to-end encrypted messaging.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared for third-party marketing. Service providers receive the information needed to operate the messaging service; consent is not transferable to another sender.

Website hosting

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, advertising scripts, or analytics added by Austin. GitHub may collect technical request information when serving the pages under its own privacy statement.

Retention and requests

Conversation records may be retained for continuity, inquiry handling, troubleshooting, and applicable obligations. No automatic deletion interval is promised. Contact me@austincrane.us to request access, correction, or deletion of records controlled by Austin. Records held by providers are subject to their retention practices and applicable requirements.

Your choices

You can stop SMS replies by replying STOP. Opting out does not automatically delete prior conversation records. Do not send passwords, API keys, payment details, or other sensitive information to the assistant.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Austin Crane at me@austincrane.us. Updates will be published here with a revised effective date.
